## Byte-ing Beavers Critique

### What are some aspects of the project that you like, and why?
I love the idea of an organized place to find summer housing so that I do not have to scroll through thousands of websites to find a summer house. I also love the idea of incorporating fraternities and dorms into the website as it is very unique and an underrated place to stay during the summer. I like that the user can save housing for later and get notifications for updates. I also like the scope of the project because only allowing MIT students makes it less complicated and maybe allow in the future or other universities. I also love the colors from the Visual Design Study.
### What are some aspects of the project that you wish were different in some way?
I love the reviews concept for each place but I think it could be helpful to maybe be able to reach out to a person who previously lived in a housing and allow the user to ask questions to the specific person. I also think that at least 4-5 pictures of the housing should be required. Also I think that the roommate posting concept should require pictures of the person so that people can really know who they are talking to. Also do you need a concept for filtering/displaying the potential housing or how will you incorporate it? I see in the listing concept, there is a set of amenities with distance as an instance, will you create and delete amenities based on the distance from the work or bus?
### What are some aspects you are wondering about; perhaps some suggestions for a simplification, a different approach, or whatever?
I was wondering how the user will actually book their summer housing, like if it was an apartment, will they have to book it through a different website and still consult the landlord? Also there are a lot of legal fine prints when it comes to booking a summer housing so will the website take care of it? Are you going to work with the MIT Summer Housing for this? Also how will apartments or subleases be incentivized to put up their lease here?
