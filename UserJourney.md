# User Journey

Alex, a full-stack developer at a small startup, begins a new project and dreads the inevitable slog of rebuilding backend essentials like authentication, profiles, and file downloads from scratch. Searching for a better approach, Alex discovers Conceptual, signs up, and immediately starts browsing modular backend “concepts” uploaded by other developers. After exploring trending components and liking a few, Alex inspects a popular Authentication concept, checks its download history, and decides it’s reliable. With one click, Alex downloads the concept package, automatically adding it to their profile’s downloaded items. Alex then opens the integrated request playground, begins a development session, and prompts an LLM to generate a new backend for their project by composing these downloaded concepts rather than rewriting everything by hand. In less than half an hour, Alex has a functioning skeleton backend powered by proven, reusable concepts — a process that previously took days — and leaves Conceptual excited to contribute their own improved version back to the community.
